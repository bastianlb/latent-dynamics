## Verdict

The rebound runs on optimizer time and overfitting runs on data passes. The H1 total minimum sits at step 7 to 8 and half recovery at step 56 to 70 at every N from 250 to 60000, while gap onset moves from step 85 to 266 to 1400 and never happens at N=60000. Across N the step-clock ratio of the largest to smallest median is 1.07 for e_min and 1.24 for e50, against 234 and 189 in epochs. The rebound is not overfitting. At N=1000 H1 total hits its minimum at epoch 0.94 and is half recovered by epoch 8.7, when the test/train gap is still 0.8% (onset of a 10% gap is epoch 33, 4.1x later than e50). The linear AE never overfits and still shows a relative rebound of 0.58 at N=1000 against 0.61 for the MLP AE. Across the 48 MLP runs the final rebound has no rank correlation with the final gap (rho -0.08, within-N 0.14) or with log N (0.08), while the gap itself falls steeply with log N (rho -0.97). Weight decay 1e-4 cuts the N=1000 test excess to 0.42x and the gap to 0.74x and leaves the final rebound unchanged (ratio 1.1). Within a run the rebound correlates with the gap (median rho 0.88) but equally with train-fit progress and pr_dim (0.89), and the partial correlation of rebound with gap given pr_dim is 0.03 while pr_dim given gap stays at 0.41. The rebound starts at the same epoch as the pr_dim minimum (0.94 at N=1000) and across runs tracks the pr_dim recovery (rho 0.82 pooled, 0.44 within N and dataset). The train probe topology is indistinguishable from the test probe (relative H1 total difference 0.4% before and -1.2% after onset at N=1000), so memorization is not visible in the topology, only faintly in pr_dim and rho_err_knn.

## Caveats

- Most correlations use n=24 (one wd) or n=48 (pooled) runs, within-group ones use groups of 6 (N, wd) or 3 (N, wd, dataset). The within-N rho with test excess (0.84 pooled) is mostly a dataset effect, since Fashion-MNIST has both larger excess and lower H1 rebound, and it drops to 0.28 once dataset is controlled.
- Final rebound is set mostly by dataset (about 23 for MNIST and about 11 for Fashion-MNIST at the same N), not by N or gap.
- Topology exists only on 66 masked snapshots. e_min, e50 and e90 therefore have a resolution of one mask step, which is coarse at early epochs.
- The end value uses step > 4000 and the minimum search uses step >= 2 for every N, since epoch thresholds do not transfer across N. The last snapshot is dropped everywhere.
- Within-run time-course Spearman values share the common monotone drift of nearly every quantity after e_min, so the raw values near 0.9 are weak evidence. The partial correlations are the informative ones and even those are only rank-linear controls.
- At N=250 the train probe holds 250 points, so it is excluded from section F. At N=full no run ever exceeds a 10% gap, so no onset exists there.
- The classifier shows a nonzero rebound at N=1000 (relative 0.19, final rebound H1 total 4.1, e_min 1.6) and its test topology correlates with the loss ratio after epoch 1 (rho 0.88 for H1 total). Both are monotone drifts, not evidence of causation. The classifier across-N correlation (rho 0.66 for H1 total vs loss gap, n=30) mixes in N=16000 and is confounded by N and dataset.
- The classifier train-minus-test H1 difference anticorrelates with its loss gap (rho -0.93), which is the one place memorization is visible in topology.
- The step clock holds for e_min and e50 but not for e90. H1 total e90 is 1800 steps at N=250 against 176 to 298 at the other N, a ratio of 10. At N=250 the curve keeps creeping up while the gap explodes past 100 (see the orange curve in panel 1), so a late tail there may be overfitting related. Entropy and PMag e_min and e50 are also later and noisier than H1 total (entropy e_min about 27 steps).
- Classifier e_min at N=250 and N=4000 is occasionally very late (thousands of epochs) because the curve is flat, so its timing numbers there are unreliable.
