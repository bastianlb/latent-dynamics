# Regime metrics: takeaways (N = 1000, wd 0, test probe, mean of 3 seeds, final snapshot dropped)

Regime edges: early fit below 1 epoch, fitting 1 to 33 epochs (MLP AE gap onset 31 MNIST, 36 FM), overfitting beyond. MLP AE test-recon minimum at 120 (MNIST) and 150 (FM) epochs. Figures: dynamics_report/figures/regime_metrics.{pdf,png}, regime_traintest, regime_acrossN, regime_metrics_full.png. Table: regime_metric_table.{csv,md}.

## Autoencoder, early fit (below 1 epoch)
Almost everything moves and the latent cloud collapses. The participation ratio falls from 10 to 1.2 on MNIST and from 6.3 to 1.4 on FM. The top-2 variance share rises from 0.32 to 0.96 on MNIST. TwoNN dimension falls from 11 to 6.8 on MNIST and from 9.9 to 4.8 on FM. Neighbourhoods reorganise fast, with kNN overlap against the previous snapshot rising from about 0.2 to 0.85. Class structure does not improve yet. kNN label accuracy stays at 0.51 on MNIST and the nearest-class-mean accuracy drops from 0.49 to 0.29. Overlap with the pixel kNN graph falls from 0.21 to 0.16. The kNN distance CV rises from 0.20 to 0.36 and hubness skew falls from 1.2 to 0.45. Topology collapses in step, with the H1 lifetime sum falling from 38 to 11 on MNIST. MNIST and FM agree in sign on every metric here. The linear AE does not collapse. Its participation ratio rises from 9.4 to 12 and it only dips at about 30 epochs.

## Autoencoder, fitting (1 to 33 epochs)
The collapse reverses and the latent space acquires structure. The participation ratio regrows from 1.2 to 6.4 on MNIST and from 1.4 to 3.9 on FM. The top-2 share falls from 0.96 to 0.45. kNN label accuracy rises from 0.51 to 0.84 on MNIST and from 0.56 to 0.74 on FM. The kNN pixel-cluster accuracy rises from 0.61 to 0.86. Overlap with the pixel kNN graph jumps from 0.16 to 0.54, the largest single change of any metric in this regime. The Fisher ratio for labels only reaches 0.63 on MNIST and 1.5 on FM, far below the classifier. The kNN distance CV falls back from 0.36 to 0.25. The H1 lifetime sum rebounds from 11 to 31. Neighbourhoods are nearly frozen by the end, with previous-snapshot overlap at 0.96. The correlation of reconstruction error with kNN distance disagrees between datasets. It falls from 0.79 to 0.57 on MNIST and rises from 0.18 to 0.59 on FM. The reconstruction gap is still below 10% throughout.

## Autoencoder, overfitting (beyond 33 epochs)
Little changes in structure and the test gap opens without a loud latent signal. Label accuracy, pixel-cluster accuracy and neighbour overlap are flat within 5%, for example kNN label accuracy 0.84 to 0.86 on MNIST. Fisher ratio and silhouette decay slowly, with the Fisher ratio for labels falling from 0.63 to 0.57 on MNIST and from 1.5 to 1.2 on FM. Dimension keeps creeping up. The participation ratio goes from 6.4 to 8.4 on MNIST and from 3.9 to 4.6 on FM, and local PR dimension from 6.2 to 7.3 on MNIST. The kNN distance CV turns upward, from 0.25 to 0.27 on MNIST and from 0.31 to 0.37 on FM. The correlation between reconstruction error and kNN distance climbs from 0.57 to 0.80 on MNIST and from 0.59 to 0.75 on FM, so poorly reconstructed test points sit in sparse regions. The H1 lifetime sum is flat at 31 on MNIST and 15 on FM. MNIST and FM agree in direction except for the kNN distance CV, which rises much more on FM. The linear AE never opens a 10% gap and mostly plateaus, with the participation ratio ending at 11 on MNIST.

## Classifier
The classifier moves in the first epoch and then stops. Its participation ratio falls from 10 to 3.2 and then sits at 4.6 to 4.8 from 33 epochs on, while the AE keeps growing toward 8.4. kNN label accuracy rises from 0.52 to 0.78 at 1 epoch and to 0.89 at 33 epochs on MNIST, and is flat afterwards. FM follows at 0.61, 0.68 and 0.78. The Fisher ratio for labels reaches 2.2 on MNIST, nearly four times the AE value of 0.57. On FM it peaks near 4.8 at about 12 epochs and decays to 3.1. Pixel-cluster structure is lost. kNN pixel-cluster accuracy falls from 0.76 to 0.68 on MNIST and overlap with the pixel kNN graph stays near 0.30, against 0.58 to 0.62 for the AE. Overlap with step 0 stays at 0.11 to 0.17. The raw latent norm keeps growing, from 0.53 to 56. The only late sign of overfitting is the test-to-train nearest-neighbour distance, which rises from 1.0 to 1.1 on both datasets. Datasets agree in direction and differ in size, and the FM classifier saturates train accuracy later (41 epochs against 20).

## Train versus test probe
At N = 1000 the probes separate late and mostly in one metric. The correlation between reconstruction error and kNN distance ends at 0.80 on the MNIST test probe and 0.23 on the train probe (FM 0.75 against 0.17). The train value falls after the test minimum while the test value rises, and the two differ by more than 10% for good from epoch 74 on MNIST and 120 on FM. That is after the gap onset at 31 to 36 epochs and at the test-recon minimum. Dimension and density split by only 6 to 15%. The participation ratio ends at 9.0 on train and 8.4 on test on MNIST, and the kNN distance CV at 0.45 and 0.37 on FM. The H1 lifetime sum is the same on both probes on MNIST (31) and 15 against 13 on FM. Cross-probe nearest-neighbour distances stay within 0.97 to 1.06 of 1, so train and test points do not occupy different regions. At N = 60000 the probes coincide on every metric, for example the participation ratio is 8.9 on both for MNIST, and no gap opens. Memorisation looks like local sparsity and train-point reconstruction decoupling from geometry, not a global change of shape. Train-probe values at N = 250 (250 points) are not comparable and are not shown.

## Across N
Few metrics track the gap, and every correlation is confounded with N. The final gap falls from 91 at N = 250 to 3.2 at 1000, 0.52 at 4000 and 0.001 at full data on MNIST. Its Spearman correlation with log N across the 24 MLP AE wd0 runs is -0.97, so any metric that is monotone in N also tracks the gap. Previous-snapshot kNN overlap correlates 0.96 with the gap and -0.97 with log N. Overlap with the pixel kNN graph correlates -0.90 with the gap and 0.90 with log N, rising from 0.54 at N = 250 to 0.67 at full data on MNIST. Cross-probe train-to-test distance gives 0.89. The participation ratio, kNN pixel-cluster accuracy, Fisher ratio, hubness skew and the three topology metrics are not related to the gap (absolute correlation at most 0.35). The H1 lifetime sum is 32, 31, 33 and 32 on MNIST from N = 250 to full. The kNN distance CV is non-monotone and peaks at N = 1000 (0.27 MNIST, 0.37 FM). The error-distance correlation falls with N on FM (0.70 to 0.48) and stays near 0.78 on MNIST. Weight decay 1e-4 changes end values by less than 10% for most metrics and does not change the ordering. The N = 60000 end value uses the last 10 snapshots at about 40 epochs, not epochs above 500, so it is not strictly comparable.

## Metrics that best distinguish the regimes (AE)
1. Previous-snapshot kNN overlap (0.2 to 0.85 in early fit, 0.96 at the end of fitting, flat afterwards).
2. Participation ratio (collapse to 1.2 at 1 epoch, regrowth through fitting, slow creep afterwards).
3. Overlap with the pixel kNN graph (falls in early fit, jumps from 0.16 to 0.54 in fitting, flat in overfitting).
4. Top-2 variance share (0.32 to 0.96 to 0.45 across early fit and fitting).
5. Correlation of reconstruction error with kNN distance (the only metric that clearly moves in the overfitting regime and splits train from test).

## Metrics that best distinguish classifier from AE
1. Overlap with the pixel kNN graph (classifier 0.30 flat, AE 0.58 to 0.62 on MNIST and FM).
2. Fisher ratio for labels (classifier 2.2 against AE 0.57 on MNIST).
3. Test-to-train nearest-neighbour distance (classifier 1.1 at the end, AE 1.0 or below).
