# AE latent-dynamics grid: findings (MNIST, Adam lr1e-3, bs128, 20k steps; mean ± sd over 3 seeds)

Frame: generalization (train-vs-test reconstruction gap) tied to latent geometry; encoder never sees labels. Labels/clusters are annotations only.
Figures (analysis/figures/): findings_generalization.png, findings_memorization.png, findings_linear_theory.png, findings_class_vfield.png (plus the older ts_*/bars_* figs).
New code: /homes/lbastian/latent-dynamics/ae_memorization.py -> analysis/memorization/ (per-run npz/json, results.json). Epoch = step*128/N.

## 0. Baseline effect that matters everywhere
MNIST test is intrinsically ~1.6% easier than train for a d=16 subspace: PCA-16 (fit on 60k) MSE test/train-60k = -1.6% (d8: -1.1%). Linear AE at full N gives -1.7%, N=4000 -1.6%. So "gap" ~ -1.6% means NO overfit; excess gap = (1+gap)/(1-0.016)-1.
Train recon is on the N-pool (N<=4000) or a fixed 10k train subset (full; subset noise ~0.3% rel); test is the full 10k test split. Relative gap te/tr-1 explodes when train->0 (N=250: 486±180), so also report absolute te-tr.

## 1. Generalization (figure findings_generalization.png)
Final test / train / abs gap, MLP d16 wd0: N=250 0.559±0.008 / 0.0013±0.0006 / 0.557; N=1000 0.375±0.005 / 0.068±0.001 / 0.307; N=4000 0.188±0.001 / 0.121±0.003 / 0.067 (rel 0.55±0.04); full 0.132 / 0.131 / +0.001 (rel +0.5±0.5%, i.e. ~+2% excess over baseline; ~within noise).
- Scaling: abs gap ~N^-0.4 between 250 and 1000 (train hits ~0, test saturates at ~64% of the init MSE of ~0.87) but ~N^-1.1 between 1000 and 4000 and ~0 at full. Not a single power law; saturation at small N.
- wd=1e-4 (Adam coupled L2) lowers final test error and gap: N=1000 test 0.293±0.003 (gap 0.22; rel 3.0±0.1 vs 4.5±0.1), N=4000 0.162 (rel 0.34±0.00 vs 0.55), N=250 rel 195±87 vs 486±180. At full N no effect (-0.3±0.4% vs +0.5±0.5%, noise). wd barely changes the BEST test error (N=1000: 0.244 vs 0.247) - it removes the late rise.
- d (N=1000, wd0): rel gap d8 4.37±0.20, d16 4.52±0.13, d64 3.90±0.08; d8 vs d16 within noise, d64 modestly lower. Absolute test error falls with d (0.468/0.375/0.235). Full N: gap ~0 for all d (d8 +2.5±0.3%, i.e. ~+3.7% over its -1.1% baseline: mild).
- Linear AE (d16, wd0): rel gap N=250 +18.2±0.2%, N=1000 +2.8±0.1%, N=4000 -1.6±0.3%, full -1.7±0.2%. Excess over baseline 20.2% / 4.4% / ~0 / ~0: ~1/N (N*excess 50 vs 44) until the sample-PCA error vanishes; no "memorization", test is monotone (rise from test-min <0.03%).
- When does the gap open (MLP, wd0)? Relative gap first >10%: N=250 at epoch 41±2 (81 steps), N=1000 at 31±1 (246 steps), N=4000 at 50±6 (1559 steps), d8/d64 N=1000 at 29/35 epochs. Epoch-scale (~30-50 epochs), not step-scale, matches the classifier's 20-40 epoch memorization. Linear N=250 opens at ~230 epochs.
- Test-recon minimum then rise (AE overfitting peak) EXISTS for MLP: min at epoch ~123 (N=250, step 240), ~122 (N=1000, step 956±0), ~227 (N=4000, step 7105±490). Final/min-1: N=250 +63±4%, N=1000 +52±1% (wd1e-4: +20±1%), N=4000 +4.3±0.8% (wd1e-4: +0.2%, ~none), full 0. Caveat: snapshots are log-spaced (~9%/snapshot), so min location is +-1 snapshot; the "same epoch ~122 for N=250 and 1000" coincidence rests on that resolution.

## 2. Memorization in latent space (figure findings_memorization.png)
Method note: train probe has N points (250 at N=250) vs 1000 test points, so all ratios use matched-size, label-stratified test subsamples and unbiased pooled within-variance (ae_memorization.py).
Final train/test ratios (matched; spread_lab | total var | white var | test->train NN ratio):
- MLP N=250 wd0: 1.20±0.04 | 1.16±0.03 | 1.26±0.02 | 0.87±0.02 ; N=1000: 1.11 | 1.06 | 1.13 | 1.02±0.02 ; N=4000: 1.035 | 1.02 | 1.05 | 1.00 ; full: 1.02 | 1.01 | 1.03 | 1.00.
- Linear N=250: 1.17±0.01 | 1.12±0.02 | 1.22±0.03 | 0.98±0.03 (gap 18%, no memorization).
- Classifier N=250: spread_lab 0.83±0.08 (wd1e-4: 0.44±0.02), km spread 1.16, total var 1.14, NN ratio 1.17±0.06 (wd1e-4 1.52±0.15), dgm ratio 0.89 (0.67).
Sign: AE train latents are MORE spread (not collapsed) than test; classifier train is class-collapsed (lab spread <1, NN ratio >1). Contrast holds, but see the artefact below.
ARTEFACT / reference: projecting the same probe images on the top-16 PCs fitted on the N-point train pool (no network) gives spread_lab 1.172±0.008, var ratio 1.123±0.013 at N=250 (1.055/1.019 at N=1000, 1.01/0.99 at N=4000). The linear AE at N=250 reproduces exactly this (1.17/1.12): it is a train-fitted-subspace effect (sample-PC overfit), not memorization. The MLP exceeds the reference only by ~+0.03 (N=250), +0.05 (N=1000), +0.025 (N=4000). The geometric signature is therefore small and does NOT scale with memorization depth (N=250: MLP train err 0.001 vs linear 0.26 yet spread 1.20 vs 1.17). The classifier also shows total-var 1.14 at N=250 (same artefact); its distinctive signal is lab spread <1 and NN ratio >1. The only AE-specific geometric signature: NN ratio 0.87±0.02 at N=250 (train points repel; develops after epoch ~40, vs ~0.97-1.0 for linear AE and N>=1000) - a 3-seed result.
- Time course (MLP N=250): train/test white-var ratio 1.10 already at epoch 1 (init/sampling offset; also seen for the classifier at 1.12) rises to 1.29 by epoch 160; NN ratio departs from 1 only after epoch ~80 (0.94) -> geometry lags the recon gap (opens at 41 epochs) rather than leading it. White var ratio is unreliable at d=64 (0.69-0.84; covariance estimated from 1000 pts) and is biased ~+1.7% at d16.
- Per-sample memorization: t_mem = first step with err_i < 0.5*min_t mean(test err). Fraction memorized at end: N=250 100%, N=1000 99.9%, N=4000 27.7±1% (vs 12.4% of TEST points already below that threshold: excess ~15%), full 13.1% (test 12.3%: no excess), linear N=250 11.2% (test 7.1%). Median t_mem epoch (q10-q90): N=250 129 (66-182), N=1000 233 (45-547), N=4000 89 (9-446). Wave is narrow at N=250 (all within epochs 60-180, right at the test minimum ~123) and broad at larger N.
- Which are memorized first: easy ones. Spearman(t_mem, err at the test-min step) 0.97 (N=250), 0.92 (1000), 0.96 (4000) - but this is nearly tautological (same error ordering). Less circular: isolation in pixel space (mean dist to 5 nearest train probe points) 0.45±0.03 (N=250), 0.68 (1000), 0.81 (4000); ink |x|^2 only 0.04 (250), 0.24 (1000), 0.49 (4000). Class 1 is memorized first at N=250 (median epoch 50 vs 130-160 others). Isolated/hard images are memorized last, i.e. the memorization order is largely the generalization-difficulty order (caveat: pixel-space density is computed within the 1000 probe points).
- Kinematics: median normalised per-interval latent displacement ratio train/test 1.00±0.02 and path-length ratio 1.00±0.02 in all configs; net displacement ratio 1.05 (MLP N=250), 1.03 (linear), 1.05 (classifier): memorized points do NOT move differently in aggregate; within-train, late-memorized points travel further at large N (path length 21 vs 17 at N=4000/full) but that is the difficulty confound (same correlation for test points) and vanishes at N=250 (rho 0.05).
- Clean memorization signature: decoupling of error from latent position. Spearman(log final err, LOO distance to class mean) for TRAIN vs TEST points: N=250 -0.24±0.18 vs +0.54±0.01; N=1000 +0.03±0.03 vs +0.70±0.04; N=4000 0.56 vs 0.62; full 0.52 vs 0.56; linear N=250 0.36 vs 0.36. Once train points are memorized their error no longer depends on where they sit relative to class structure, while test errors still do. (Train err~1e-3 at N=250 is noisy, so the N=250 value is weakly informative; N=1000 train err 0.07 is the better datum.)

## 3. Does geometry track the gap beyond N? (MLP d16, 24 runs; target log(te/tr))
Raw Spearman across configs is huge (|rho| 0.9-0.95 for spread, var, dgm ratio, kNN overlap, lab kNN) but this is N. Partial on (log N, family, wd): spread_lab +0.59, var +0.67, kNN-overlap -0.59, effrank -0.72 when full-N (gap~0) is included; restricted to N<=4000 (18 runs) all |partial r| <=0.2 except white-var -0.46 (wrong sign); within-config seed-level rho: spread_lab +0.24, spread_km +0.25, nn_ratio +0.25, all others |rho|<0.4 (n=24, |rho|>0.4 needed for p<0.05). With only 8 configs these cannot resolve a signal; conclusion: nothing beats log N. Relative-gap target gives the same picture (only tr_recon_tail10 within-config +0.62, n=18, unreplicated, treat as noise-level).

## 4. Linear-AE theory (figure findings_linear_theory.png)
Order: rise-order Spearman (PC index vs rise step, top-16 PCs, all 16 learned): linear AE 0.93±0.00 (N=1000), 0.93±0.01 (250), 0.92±0.03 (4000), 0.89±0.07 (full) - sequential, with local swaps among near-degenerate PCs. MLP d16: 0.56±0.23 (full), 0.64±0.23 (N=1000), 0.62±0.28 (4000), 0.44±0.14 (250), 0.21±0.17 (250 wd1e-4); MLP d64 full 0.92±0.01 but d64 N=1000 0.53; d8 0.46-0.69. So the MLP is only weakly/noisily sequential, except in the wide d64 full case.
Timing: log rise step vs log lambda_k slope is -1.93±0.33 (linear full), -2.0±0.3 (N=1000/4000), -1.92±0.13 (250): about -2, NOT the -1 of gradient-flow theory (t ~ ln(lambda/u0^2)/lambda). Likely Adam (per-parameter normalization, not gradient flow) and non-tiny default init; MLP slopes -1.4..-2.3 with sd 0.5-1.2. t_last/t_first ~50-90. So ordering is confirmed, 1/lambda scaling is not (steeper). Rise times come from a 50%-of-gain threshold on affine-readout R2 (baseline-subtracted); the 'tiny' legacy runs have only 60 snapshots and no run dirs, not used.
Linear AE decoder/encoder subspace -> PCA subspace: final mean cos^2 ~1.00 at N>=1000, 0.989 at N=250 (sample-PCA offset).

## 5. Vector-field decomposition (figure findings_class_vfield.png; held-out R2, last quarter of intervals)
Global affine R1 (late): AE MLP full 0.74±0.01 (N=250 0.92, N=1000 0.79, 4000 0.83); classifier full 0.54±0.03 (N=250 0.97, 1000 0.97, 4000 0.98 at wd0; 0.73-0.82 with wd1e-4). Group-affine share of the global-affine residual, (R2-R1)/(1-R1), test probe, full N: AE label 0.179±0.007, input-cluster (km) 0.192±0.002; classifier label 0.170±0.014, km 0.134±0.010. So for the AE labels are NOT special (km >= lab at every N; grp ~0.15-0.19 for N>=1000 with 10 groups of ANY input clustering); for the classifier labels beat km modestly (0.17 vs 0.13; N=1000 train 0.28 vs 0.17). Nonlinear kernel on top adds only +0.03-0.05 (AE) / +0.01-0.03 (classifier) of residual; shuffled-residual null is -0.07, so these small gains are real but small. Residual mostly unpredictable (>75%).
Artefact: train-probe group gains at N=250 collapse (AE 0.02, classifier 0.06/-0.01) because the 250-pt train probe gives ~12 pts/group per split; same for AE and classifier, so NOT a memorization signature. d64 AE has much smaller group gain (0.04-0.09).

Test-probe final, full N, MLP AE vs classifier: NCM label 0.816±0.008 vs 0.971±0.003; NCM km 0.881±0.013 vs 0.617±0.006; kNN label 0.813±0.006 vs 0.969±0.005; kNN km 0.749±0.013 vs 0.583±0.007; Fisher (Sw/Sb) label 1.57±0.06 vs 0.25±0.01. Reference: random-init encoder NCM lab 0.49±0.03, km 0.52, kNN lab 0.38; raw-pixel LOO kNN lab 0.761±0.009 / km 0.760. AE label kNN exceeds pixel space only modestly (+0.05 MLP; linear AE 0.775 = pixel, within noise). The AE retains/strengthens the input-cluster structure (km NCM 0.88-0.95; linear 0.95) while the classifier erases it (0.62) and builds label structure. AE label structure at small N is BELOW pixel kNN (N=250 0.71±0.01; N=1000 0.74) - memorization costs class geometry; wd1e-4 changes it little (N=250 0.711 vs 0.710). Timing: label NCM half-gain epoch AE full 0.24±0.04 (km 0.13) vs classifier 0.043±0.011 (km 0.003) i.e. ~5x later for AE, and km structure appears before label structure in both. Train-vs-test AE NCM/kNN differences at N=250 (tr kNN 0.56 vs te 0.71) are dominated by n=250 vs 1000 probe size; classifier tr kNN 0.90 vs te 0.74 is a real train collapse.

## Caveats
- Only 3 seeds, 8 MLP configs; N=250 train err ~1e-3 makes relative gaps noisy (sd 40%). Several quantities depend on 250- vs 1000-point probes (handled by matching for geometry but not for kNN/NCM/vfield tr numbers).
- Latents are defined up to a gauge; only ratios/affine-invariant quantities used; effective rank and spread depend on anisotropic scaling (var_ratio_white is the only fully GL(d)-invariant one and is biased/noisy at d64).
- Matched-size geometry is compared with a train-fit PCA reference; excess over it is small and was not tested against more than the d16 case.
- Adam, not gradient flow, default init; 20k steps = only 43 epochs at full N (no late overfit possible there). Test-min location is quantised by log snapshot spacing.
- t_mem thresholds are relative to the run's own best test level; difficulty and memorization order are confounded by construction.

## Recommended next steps (generalization framing)
1. Strongest and cheapest: the test-min/overfit-peak at epoch ~120 and the gap opening at 30-50 epochs are N-independent in epochs; a dense (per-step) window around epochs 20-200 at N=1000 (a few seeds) would resolve whether geometry leads or lags the recon gap (currently lags). Justified.
2. Use the pixel-PCA/sample-subspace reference as a null for every train-vs-test geometry claim, and add a held-out-but-same-size "pseudo-train" probe (train subset resampling) to separate sample-size from memorization effects.
3. NTK/linearization analysis: justified only for the linear-AE question (why slope -2 not -1: test with SGD vs Adam and small init, cheap); low priority for the MLP AE since PC ordering is already weak there.
4. Fashion-MNIST replication: worthwhile only for the cheap, robust results (epoch-scale gap onset, wd removing the late rise, lab-vs-km class structure contrast AE vs classifier, vfield lab==km for AE). Not worth it for the geometry-tracks-gap claim, which is currently null.
5. Add a bigger-N sweep between 4000 and full (e.g. 16000) and 5+ seeds so within-N geometry-vs-gap correlations are testable.
